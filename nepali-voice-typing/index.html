Nepali Voice Typing (नेपाली भ्वाइस टाइपिङ) — Speech to Text

Free Nepali Voice Typing tool

Speak in Nepali and watch your words appear instantly as नेपाली युनिकोड text. There is no faster way to write in Nepali — no keyboard layout to learn, no romanized spelling to remember. Just tap the microphone, talk naturally, and this Nepali speech-to-text tool types for you, up to three times faster than typing by hand.

The tool uses your browser's built-in speech recognition with a high-quality Nepali (ne-NP) language model, so it understands natural Nepali speech including common accents. The recognized text is standard Nepali Unicode (Devanagari), ready to paste into Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, email, Word, Excel or any official document. You can also send it straight to our Unicode to Preeti converter with one click — perfect for designers who need Preeti font output.

How to use Nepali voice typing?

  1. Tap the round microphone button in the corner of the typing box and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  2. Speak clearly in Nepali — your words appear instantly, word by word, as you talk. The tool keeps listening while you speak, and switches the mic off automatically after 1 minute of silence.
  3. Say «पूर्ण विराम» to insert the Nepali full stop (।), «नयाँ लाइन» to start a new line, or «प्रश्न चिन्ह» for a question mark.
  4. Made a mistake? Tap Undo voice to remove the last spoken phrase, or click anywhere in the text and speak — new words are inserted right at your cursor.
  5. When you are done, tap Copy, or To Preeti to convert your speech into Preeti font instantly.

Your text is saved automatically in your browser, so nothing is lost even if the page reloads. No audio or text is ever stored on our servers.
