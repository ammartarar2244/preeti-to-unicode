English to Nepali Typing (रोमनबाट नेपाली टाइपिङ)

Free Online Nepali Unicode Typing Tool

If you’re looking for an easy way to type in Nepali, this free English to Nepali typing tool is just what you need. Type Nepali words the way they sound in English (Roman) — like kasto chha — and they are converted into नेपाली युनिकोड (कस्तो छ) instantly, right as you type.

This Nepali typing tool shows all the possible suggestions after typing just a few letters. You can pick the best match with a single click, the arrow keys, or the number keys — which already helps many bloggers, students and content writers to write anything in Nepali without learning a Nepali keyboard layout.

The converted text is standard Nepali Unicode, so you can copy it and paste it anywhere — Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, email, Word, Excel or any official document. Preeti To Unicode Converter also lets you convert traditional Preeti font to Unicode and vice versa on the same site.

How to type Nepali with this tool?

Just write Nepali words using English letters and press Space — the word converts to Nepali automatically. For example:

  • mero desh nepal ho → मेरो देश नेपाल हो
  • namaste → नमस्ते
  • timilai kasto chha → तिमीलाई कस्तो छ

While typing, suggestions appear below the box. Use the ↑ ↓ arrow keys or Tab to highlight a different suggestion, press Enter or a number key (1–6) to choose it, and press Esc if you want to keep the word in English. To type a full English word or sentence without conversion, press Ctrl+G or tap the नेपाली/ABC switch. Your text is saved automatically in your browser, so nothing is lost even if the page reloads.
