Privacy Policy

How we protect your typed content and guarantee security.

At **NepaliTools**, we are committed to user privacy. Our tools are designed to operate entirely client-side.

1. Client-Side Calculations Only

Every time you type, convert, or practice Nepali typing, all processing occurs locally in your browser sandbox. We do not transmit your inputs, text content, or files to any remote servers. Your data stays on your device.

2. Local Cache & Cookies

We use browser local storage to save your visual preference (Light / Dark Mode). This does not contain any personal identifier.
