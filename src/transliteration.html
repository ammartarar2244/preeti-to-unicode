Instant Nepali Transliteration (Phonetic Roman to Unicode)

Nepali Transliteration is the fastest way to write in Nepali without memorizing traditional keyboard layouts like Preeti or Kantipur. Instead of pressing unrelated keys to find letters, you simply type the English phonetic spelling of the word (for example, typing nepal outputs नेपाल).

All text generated by our transliterator uses universal Devanagari Unicode, ensuring that your text never appears corrupted, garbled, or broken across Android, iOS, Windows, Mac, or social media.
