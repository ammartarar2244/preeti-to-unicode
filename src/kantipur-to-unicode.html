How to Convert Kantipur Font to Unicode Online

The Kantipur font has been a staple in Nepalese print media and desktop publishing workflows. However, sharing files written in Kantipur requires the recipient to have the same font installed, which is not suitable for search engines or web platforms. Converting your Kantipur content to standard Devanagari Unicode solves this incompatibility.

Conversion Steps:

  1. Copy the text from your legacy document (MS Word, InDesign, etc.).
  2. Paste the text into the Kantipur Font Input box above.
  3. The converter will automatically output standard Devanagari in the right pane.
  4. Click Copy Unicode to copy it to your clipboard.
