Kantipur Font vs Preeti Font: What Is the Difference?

While Kantipur and Preeti share a very similar typewriter keyboard layout, Kantipur features custom typographic weight distributions, tighter letter spacing (kerning), and distinctive conjunct glyph designs favored by major Kathmandu newspaper houses. However, both fonts suffer from the same digital drawback: they are 8-bit ASCII fonts. When transferred to a computer without the Kantipur.ttf file installed, the text appears as random English characters (e.g. sflGtk'/ instead of कान्तिपुर). Unicode assigns standard global hexadecimal code points (U+0900 to U+097F) so your words display reliably on iPhones, Androids, and Windows PCs.

How Our Engine Solves Vowel Reordering & Ligature Glitches

In traditional Kantipur typesetting, the short "i" matra (ह्रस्व इकार 'ि') is physically typed before the consonant (e.g., l + s = कि). In logical Unicode Devanagari, the consonant must precede the combining vowel mark. Our converter uses lookahead regex parsing to swap visual prefixes into logical linguistic order, fix complex halant clusters (क्, त्, म्), and properly attach the Reph (र्) and anusvara (ं) diacritics.
